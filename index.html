THE PROBLEM

Every second of the golden window counts.

Traditional ambulance response delays critical care during asthma attacks, cardiac events, and accidents — while patients wait, conditions deteriorate. Most of that time is lost before help even leaves the ground.

8–12

Minutes, on average

HOW IT WORKS

From open tab to airborne, in four steps.

01

Choose the emergency

Asthma attack, cardiac support, or accident — each routes a different payload and response protocol.

02

Location, shared automatically

The moment an emergency is chosen, your device location is requested — no typing an address under pressure.

03

Drone launches, ambulance alerted

The nearest drone is dispatched immediately. If the case is critical, a nearby ambulance is notified in parallel.

04

Live tracking, start to finish

A three-minute countdown and live drone position stay on screen until the payload is delivered.
